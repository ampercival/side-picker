        How it Works

        1. Game & factions: Pick a saved game or type the faction names yourself.

        2. Add players: Enter everyone's name. Each one gets a card.

        3. Rank each player's factions — drag them or use the buttons:
        • Move factions a player wants into Preferences (green); the top one counts most.
        • Move factions they don't want into Banned (red).
        • Leave the rest in Available.

        4. Collect picks live (optional): Click Live Room and share the link. Players rank their own factions on their phones and submit — their picks appear here in real time.

        5. Optimize: Click Optimize & Assign and choose a goal — Highest Group Score (best total) or Fairest for Everyone (lifts the unluckiest player). Use Share Results Link to send everyone the final assignments.

        Everything saves to your Sessions automatically — reopen a game night anytime. Tip: "Lock" a player to keep their picks safe when you hit Randomize.
