        How it works

        1. Game and factions: Start from a saved game, or type the faction names yourself.

        2. Add players: Enter everyone's name. Each one gets a card.

        3. Rank each player's factions — drag them or use the buttons:
        • Move factions a player wants into Preferences (green); the top one counts most.
        • Move factions they don't want into Banned (red).
        • Leave the rest in Available.
        • Choose the same rank to tie factions. On phones, drag from the dotted grip; swipe elsewhere to scroll.

        4. Collect picks live (optional): Click Live room and send each person their own player link, or turn on joining and share the group link so people add themselves. Players rank their factions on their phones and submit; their picks appear here within a few seconds.

        5. Assign: Click Compare and assign to see both goals: Highest Group Score (best total) or Fairest for Everyone (protects the lowest-scoring player). Publish one, then use Share results to send everyone the assignments.

        Everything saves automatically, so you can reopen a session anytime. Lock a player to keep their choices when you use Randomize choices.
